# University of Toronto (U of T) PhD in Computer Science: Direct from Bachelor's Route

The University of Toronto is routinely ranked among the top 10 Computer Science programs globally. Because of this, their Direct-Entry PhD program is highly competitive, often compared directly to top-tier US institutions like MIT and Stanford.

## 🎓 Academic Requirements (The 5-Year Direct-Entry PhD)

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> **The 5-Year Program Structure**  
> If you are admitted directly from a Bachelor's degree (bypassing the Master's), you are placed into the **5-Year Direct-Entry PhD**. This requires double the coursework (4.0 full-course equivalents instead of 2.0) compared to students entering with a Master's degree, ensuring you get the foundational graduate training before diving fully into your thesis.

- **Academic Standing:** The absolute minimum requirement is an **A- average** in your relevant undergraduate courses. Your First-Class Honours and 4.53/5.00 CGPA from Ahmadu Bello University places you well above this baseline.
- **Research Potential:** U of T expects "exceptional promise." Since you are skipping the Master's, your application must heavily emphasize your founding engineer roles, your global competition wins (Huawei, MATLAB), and any independent research or publications.
- **Pre-Application Networking:** In the U of T Computer Science department, you are generally expected to identify and connect with a potential supervisor **before** applying. A faculty member must be willing to champion your application for you to be admitted.

## 📝 Application Requirements & Core Documents

1. **Statement of Purpose:** Needs to be highly tailored to the specific U of T faculty members you want to work with.
2. **Comprehensive CV:** Highlighting your edge AI and hardware-constrained ML experience.
3. **Three (3) Academic References:** Note that U of T typically requires three references, unlike the UK/HK standard of two.
4. **English Language Proficiency:** You must submit official TOEFL or IELTS scores unless your university provides official documentation that English was the sole language of instruction.
5. **GRE:** The GRE is generally **NOT required** for the Computer Science PhD at U of T, though strong scores can optionally be submitted to bolster an application.

# Funding & The Connaught International Scholarship

One of the biggest advantages of the University of Toronto is that **all full-time, research-stream PhD students are guaranteed funding.** 

## 💰 The Base Funding Package
If admitted, U of T guarantees funding for all 5 years of your Direct-Entry PhD. 
- **What it covers:** Full tuition and mandatory fees, plus a base living allowance.
- **How it's paid:** This is usually a combination of a Research Assistantship (RAship) from your supervisor's grants, a Teaching Assistantship (TAship), and a Departmental Fellowship.

## 🏆 The Connaught International Scholarship
As an international student, the ultimate goal is the Connaught International Scholarship.
- **The Value:** It provides an additional **$10,000 CAD annual "top-up"** on top of your base funding package, renewable for up to 5 years.
- **How to Apply:** There is **NO separate application**. It is entirely nomination-based. When you apply for the PhD, the Department of Computer Science reviews all incoming international applications and nominates the absolute best candidates to the School of Graduate Studies. 
- **Strategy:** You must explicitly tell your prospective supervisor that you are highly interested in being nominated for merit-based entrance awards like the Connaught.

## 📅 Application Process & Timeline (Entry Fall 2027)

- **Applications Open:** Usually early Fall (September/October 2026).
- **Application Deadline:** Early December 2026 (typically the first week of December).
- **Outcome Notification:** Offers are usually made between February and April 2027.

# 🧑‍🏫 Navigating the U of T Faculty

U of T is a powerhouse in Machine Learning (it is the academic home of Geoffrey Hinton, the "Godfather of AI"). You will find massive research groups dedicated to Machine Learning, Systems, and AI.

**What to look for:**
Given your interest in **Edge AI, Federated Learning, and constrained hardware**, you should look into the **Computer Systems and Networks** research group, as well as the **Machine Learning** group. U of T has strong faculty working on distributed machine learning, systems for ML, and privacy-preserving AI.

**Next Step:** Go to the U of T Computer Science faculty directory, filter by "Machine Learning" and "Systems," and find professors whose recent publications focus on Federated Learning or Edge AI. Email them your CV and a brief research proposal *before* December!
