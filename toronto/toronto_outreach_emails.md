# University of Toronto PhD Outreach Emails

## 1. Email to Prof. Baochun Li
**Focus:** Federated Learning Frameworks for Edge Devices and Production Systems

**Subject:** Prospective PhD Student (2027) – Federated Learning Frameworks for Agricultural Edge Inference

Dear Prof. Li,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining your research group as a PhD student. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My engineering background focuses on building ML systems for unpredictable and severely constrained environments, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed entirely offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. While the inference is robust, continuously improving the model with real-world field data across smallholder farmers presents a structural bottleneck: we cannot reliably transmit gigabytes of crop imagery from rural farms over unstable 2G/3G networks to a central server.

I have been following your extensive work on distributed machine learning, specifically the development of the "Plato" framework. The need for open-source, scalable federated learning frameworks designed for production systems is critical. For my PhD, I want to investigate how frameworks like Plato can be adapted and optimized for extreme edge environments—specifically addressing the challenge of asynchronous model updates from heterogeneous, low-power devices operating on highly unstable rural networks.

Given my practical experience deploying offline models and my strong background in software engineering (as the founding engineer of KodaTrade), I am deeply interested in the systems-level challenges of federated learning that your lab focuses on. My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App).

Are you accepting new students for the upcoming cycle, and would investigating federated learning scalability under severe network constraints align with your current research focus?

Thank you for your time,
Hadiza Mohammed
