# University of Toronto: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The University of Toronto Department of Computer Science (DCS) and the School of Graduate Studies (SGS) organize virtual graduate information webinars each autumn. These sessions walk candidates through the 5-year Direct-Entry PhD program, faculty research groups, guaranteed base funding, and department nominations for the Connaught International Scholarship.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. School of Graduate Studies (SGS) Virtual Information Sessions
- **Dates:** Early to Mid-October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar Series
- **Target Audience:** International applicants across all graduate disciplines
- **Description:** Central university webinars explaining graduate school admissions policies, transcript evaluation, language proficiency exemptions, and student visa processes for Canada.

### 2. Department of Computer Science (DCS) Graduate Admissions Webinar
- **Dates:** Late October - Early November 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Session with Faculty & Graduate Office
- **Target Audience:** Prospective Direct-Entry PhD and MSc candidates
- **Description:** Dedicated DCS admissions webinar hosted by faculty members and graduate affairs staff. Focuses on the direct-from-bachelor's admissions stream, finding faculty alignment in Machine Learning, Systems, and Edge AI, drafting a targeted statement of purpose, and guaranteed 5-year funding packages.

---

## Critical Application Deadlines (Fall 2027 Intake)
- **DCS Application Portal Opens:** October 2026
- **DCS Application Deadline (Full Consideration for Base Funding and Connaught):** Early December 2026
  - Departmental recommendation letters (3 required) and uploaded transcripts should be received promptly around the deadline.
- **Outcome Notifications:** February to April 2027
