# University of Melbourne PhD in Computer Science: Direct from Bachelor's Route

The University of Melbourne is one of Australia's premier institutions. However, the Australian PhD system has very strict structural differences compared to the US, UK, or Canada when it comes to applying directly from an undergraduate degree.

## 🎓 Academic Requirements (The "Honours" Hurdle)

> [!WARNING]  
> **The 4-Year Honours Requirement**  
> In Australia, you generally **cannot** apply for a PhD directly from a standard 3-year Bachelor's degree. You are strictly required to have an Australian 4-year Honours degree (or a Master's) that includes a substantial research component (a thesis or research project accounting for at least 25% of your final year).

- **Equivalence Assessment:** Since you graduated from Ahmadu Bello University in Nigeria, your degree will be assessed for equivalence. Most Nigerian B.Sc. or B.Eng. degrees in Computer Science are 4 to 5 years long and mandate a final year research project. If your final year project was substantial, the university may deem it equivalent to an Australian Honours degree.
- **Grade Requirement:** You need the equivalent of an "H2A" (a high second-class or first-class honours). Your First-Class Honours (4.53/5.00 CGPA) easily clears this bar.
- **Research Potential:** Just like the other programs, your undergraduate research, competition wins, and publications are vital since you are bypassing a Master's.

## 📝 Application Requirements & Core Documents

1. **Pre-Approval from a Supervisor:** At Melbourne, you are typically required to find a supervisor and get their written agreement to supervise you *before* you can formally submit your application.
2. **Research Proposal:** A detailed proposal, usually drafted with input from your prospective supervisor.
3. **Academic Transcripts & CV.**
4. **English Language Proficiency:** IELTS or TOEFL is strictly required for international students unless your degree explicitly exempts you.
5. **GRE:** The GRE is **NOT required**.

# Funding: The Melbourne Research Scholarship (MRS)

The University of Melbourne offers one massive, centralized scholarship for international students called the Graduate Research Scholarship (which encompasses the Melbourne Research Scholarship).

## 💰 What it Covers
- **Full Fee Offset:** Covers 100% of your tuition for up to 4 years.
- **Living Stipend:** An annual living allowance of approximately $37,000 AUD (~$24,000 USD).
- **Relocation Grant:** A one-time relocation allowance of $3,000 AUD for students moving from outside Australia.
- **Health Cover:** Single Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for international students.

## 📅 Application Process & Timeline (Entry 2027)

> [!TIP]  
> **Automatic Consideration**  
> There is **no separate application** for the Melbourne Research Scholarship. When you apply for admission to the PhD in Computer Science, you are automatically assessed and ranked for the scholarship based on your academic merit.

- **Application Deadlines:** Unlike other universities that have one set date, Melbourne's deadlines can be rolling or faculty-specific. However, to be considered for the major scholarship rounds for a start in early 2027, you generally need to submit your complete application (with a supervisor secured) by **October 31, 2026**.
- **Outcome Notification:** Scholarship offers are usually made progressively after the course application deadlines.

# 🎓 The Master's Pathway (Backup Plan)

If the University of Melbourne assesses your Nigerian degree and decides it is *not* equivalent to an Australian 4-year Honours degree, you will need to complete a Master's degree first. 

## How to Apply for the Master of Computer Science
Applying for the Master's is much more straightforward than the PhD because you do **not** need to find a supervisor first.

1. **Check the Math Prerequisite:** Alongside your CS degree and your excellent 4.53 CGPA, they strictly require that you completed at least 25 points (usually two semesters' worth) of university-level Mathematics or Statistics during your undergrad.
2. **English Proficiency:** You will need an IELTS score of 6.5 (with no band less than 6.0) or the TOEFL equivalent.
3. **Submit Online:** You apply directly through the University of Melbourne's online international student application portal.
4. **Documents Needed:** Just your official transcripts, your CV (highlighting your founding engineer roles and awards), and your English test scores. 
5. **Intake Dates:** They typically have two intakes: Semester 1 (starts in February/March) and Semester 2 (starts in July). You can apply for either.
