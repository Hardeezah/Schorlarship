# University of Melbourne: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The University of Melbourne and the Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology (FEIT) host online graduate research information sessions and drop-in consultations throughout the year. These sessions assist international candidates with navigating supervisor pre-approval, 4-year Honours degree equivalence, and Melbourne Research Scholarship (MRS) rankings.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. FEIT Graduate Research Virtual Information Sessions
- **Dates:** September - October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinars
- **Target Audience:** Prospective PhD and Master of Philosophy applicants in Computing and Information Systems (CIS)
- **Description:** Presentations by FEIT academic leads detailing research capabilities in Distributed Systems, Edge AI, and Machine Learning. Sessions cover supervisor outreach expectations, drafting an Australian-standard research proposal, and scholarship criteria.

### 2. Graduate Research Virtual 1-on-1 Drop-In Consultations
- **Schedule:** Running weekly across the autumn admissions period
- **Format:** Virtual / Individual Video Consultations
- **Target Audience:** Prospective international research degree candidates
- **Description:** Individual consultations with university admissions specialists to review eligibility requirements, transcripts, international qualification equivalency, and application portal procedures.

---

## Critical Application Deadlines
- **Application Window:** Rolling applications accepted year-round
- **Major International Scholarship Deadline (Starting Early 2027):** October 31, 2026
  - Complete application, formal supervisor pre-approval, and reference submissions must be completed by this date to be considered in the primary ranking round for the Melbourne Research Scholarship (MRS).
- **Outcome Notifications:** Progressively released after round closure (November 2026 to January 2027)
