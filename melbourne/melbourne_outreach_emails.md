# University of Melbourne PhD Outreach Emails

## 1. Email to A/Prof. Mingming Gong (MLR Group)
**Focus:** Adaptive Federated Learning and Domain Generalization in Constrained Environments

**Subject:** Prospective PhD Student (2027) – Adaptive Federated Learning for Constrained Agricultural Vision

Dear A/Prof. Gong,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining the Machine Learning and Reasoning (MLR) group as a PhD student. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My engineering background focuses on building robust ML systems for unpredictable, real-world environments, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed entirely offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. While the model achieves high accuracy in controlled tests, deploying it to smallholder farmers exposed two critical challenges: (1) we cannot reliably transmit large crop imagery over unstable 2G/3G networks for central training, and (2) the model suffers from severe domain shifts when encountering novel lighting conditions or camera sensors in new farms.

I have been closely following your research at the intersection of causal inference, domain generalization, and federated learning—specifically your work on "Causal Knowledge-Empowered Adaptive Federated Learning." The ability to empower edge nodes to learn causally invariant features adaptively is exactly the solution needed for agricultural deployments. For my PhD, I want to investigate how these adaptive federated frameworks can be optimized for severe uplink/downlink asymmetries, allowing edge vision models to achieve domain generalization without relying on a stable central parameter server.

Given my practical experience deploying offline models and my strong background in mathematics (recognized by my national MATLAB awards), I am deeply interested in the trustworthy machine learning challenges your group tackles. My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App).

Are you accepting new students for the upcoming cycle, and would investigating adaptive federated learning for extreme edge environments align with your current research focus?

Thank you for your time,
Hadiza Mohammed
