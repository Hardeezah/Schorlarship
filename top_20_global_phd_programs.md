# Top 20 Fully Funded Global PhD Programs for Edge AI & Machine Learning

Based on your profile (First-Class Honours, 4.53/5.00 CGPA, industrial engineering experience, and global competition wins), you are a highly competitive candidate for top-tier programs globally. 

This list is curated for **Direct-to-PhD** (or fast-track) programs that are **fully funded**, with special attention given to those that cover relocation and airfare.

---

## 🌍 Tier 1: The "Everything Covered" Mega-Funding
*These universities do not just cover tuition and stipends; they actively pay for your relocation, flights, and visas. They are aggressively recruiting top AI talent globally.*

1. **MBZUAI (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, UAE)**
   - **Why Apply:** The world’s first graduate-level, research-based AI university. Provides full tuition, monthly stipend, free accommodation, health insurance, and **annual flights home**.
   - **Direct Entry:** Yes (Note: They strictly require the GRE for Bachelor's-only applicants).

2. **OIST (Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology, Japan)**
   - **Why Apply:** A 5-year integrated PhD designed specifically for Bachelor's graduates. Operates entirely in English. They cover **relocation expenses and your flight to Japan**, plus a high standard of living.
   - **Direct Entry:** Yes, by default.

3. **University of Cambridge (United Kingdom)**
   - **Why Apply:** The **Gates Cambridge Scholarship** is one of the few UK scholarships that explicitly covers your flight, visa costs, and provides a massive stipend alongside full tuition. 
   - **Direct Entry:** Yes.

4. **National University of Singapore (NUS, Singapore)**
   - **Why Apply:** A global top 10 CS school. Through the **SINGA Scholarship**, you get full tuition, a $2,700 SGD/month stipend, a S$1,500 settling-in grant, and up to S$1,500 for your **airfare**.
   - **Direct Entry:** Yes (GRE often required for SINGA at NUS).

5. **Nanyang Technological University (NTU, Singapore)**
   - **Why Apply:** Just like NUS, NTU is heavily covered by the SINGA scholarship. Their College of Computing and Data Science is a world leader in IoT and Edge Intelligence.
   - **Direct Entry:** Yes.

6. **University of Melbourne (Australia)**
   - **Why Apply:** The Melbourne Research Scholarship covers full tuition, a massive $37,000 AUD stipend, and a **$3,000 AUD relocation grant**.
   - **Direct Entry:** Requires a Master's *or* an Honours equivalent. Your 4-5 year Nigerian degree + First Class CGPA gives you a strong chance at equivalence.

7. **Australian National University (ANU, Australia)**
   - **Why Apply:** Similar to Melbourne, the Australian Government Research Training Program (AGRTP) covers tuition, living costs, and offers **relocation and travel allowances**. ANU has elite computer vision and ML groups.
   - **Direct Entry:** Honours equivalence required.

## 💶 Tier 2: The European "Employee" Model
*In these countries, PhD candidates are not considered "students." You are hired as a full-time university employee. The salaries are so high that you can easily afford your own relocation.*

8. **EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Switzerland)**
   - **Why Apply:** PhD salaries here start around **$60,000 to $75,000 USD** per year. EPFL is arguably the best engineering school in Europe outside the UK. 
   - **Direct Entry:** Normally requires a Master's, but they offer an exclusive **"Fast-Track"** option (EDIC program) specifically for outstanding Bachelor's graduates like you.

9. **ETH Zurich (Switzerland)**
    - **Why Apply:** Consistently ranked in the global top 5 for Computer Science. Huge salaries similar to EPFL. Their systems and machine learning labs are legendary.
    - **Direct Entry:** Fast-track direct entry is possible for top-tier candidates.

10. **Max Planck Institutes (IMPRS for Computer Science, Germany)**
    - **Why Apply:** No tuition fees. You are paid a competitive German TV-L E13 salary. Max Planck is the pinnacle of pure research in Europe. 
    - **Direct Entry:** The "Preparatory Phase" allows Bachelor's students to enter directly, doing some coursework before transitioning fully to PhD research.

11. **Technical University of Munich (TUM, Germany)**
    - **Why Apply:** Germany’s top technical university, heavily invested in AI and Robotics. You are hired as a research assistant with full state benefits and salary.
    - **Direct Entry:** Possible via specific fast-track programs or Graduate Schools within the university.

12. **University of Oxford (United Kingdom)**
    - **Why Apply:** The **Clarendon Scholarship** covers full tuition and living expenses. Additionally, Oxford's EPSRC Centres for Doctoral Training (CDTs) in Autonomous Intelligent Machines and Systems are massively funded 4-year programs designed for direct Bachelor's entry.
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes.

13. **Imperial College London (United Kingdom)**
    - **Why Apply:** The President's PhD Scholarship provides full tuition and a massive £26,500/year tax-free stipend, plus a £2,000/year hardware/travel fund.
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes, via the "outstanding applicant" exception for top bachelor's graduates.

## 🍁 Tier 3: North American Heavyweights (Guaranteed Base Funding)
*Top US and Canadian universities guarantee full funding (tuition + stipend) for all 4-5 years. They do NOT cover relocation/flights, but their prestige and deep-tech industry connections (Google, Apple, Microsoft) are unmatched.*

14. **University of Toronto (Canada)**
    - **Why Apply:** The birthplace of modern Deep Learning (Geoffrey Hinton). Guaranteed 5-year funding, with the chance to win the $10,000 Connaught International Scholarship top-up.
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes, a 5-year direct-entry track.

15. **University of Waterloo (Canada)**
    - **Why Apply:** Waterloo has the best industry/co-op connections in Canada. The Cheriton School of Computer Science guarantees full funding and has incredible hardware/systems ML labs.
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes.

16. **University of British Columbia (UBC, Canada)**
    - **Why Apply:** Incredible quality of life in Vancouver, strong ML faculty, and guaranteed funding via the Four Year Fellowships (FYF).
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes.

17. **Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT, USA)**
    - **Why Apply:** The #1 engineering school in the world. The EECS PhD program is fully funded by default and you are surrounded by the best hardware/AI researchers on earth.
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes (You earn a Master's in passing).

18. **Stanford University (USA)**
    - **Why Apply:** Located in the heart of Silicon Valley. If you want to deploy Edge AI on constrained hardware, Stanford has the tightest connections to Apple, NVIDIA, and Google. Fully funded.
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes.

19. **Carnegie Mellon University (CMU, USA)**
    - **Why Apply:** Ranked #1 for Artificial Intelligence in the USA. Their School of Computer Science is massive. Fully funded.
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes.

20. **University of California, Berkeley (UC Berkeley, USA)**
    - **Why Apply:** A public Ivy and arguably the top public university globally for Computer Science. Elite AI research through BAIR (Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research). Fully funded.
    - **Direct Entry:** Yes.
