# Singapore PhD in Computer Science: NTU & NUS Direct Entry

Singapore is a global powerhouse for AI research. Its two flagship universities—**National University of Singapore (NUS)** and **Nanyang Technological University (NTU)**—both rank in the global top 15 for Computer Science. Their PhD application processes, requirements, and funding systems are so deeply intertwined that it makes sense to tackle them together.

## 🎓 Academic Requirements (Direct Entry)

Unlike the UK or Australia, both NUS and NTU are highly receptive to direct-from-Bachelor's applications, mirroring the US system.

- **Degree Standard:** The baseline requirement is a "Second Class Upper Honours" degree. Your First-Class Honours (4.53/5.00) from Ahmadu Bello University easily clears this bar and makes you highly competitive.
- **Research Potential:** Because you are skipping the Master's, your application will sink or swim based on your research potential. Your Huawei and MATLAB awards, alongside your founding engineer experience, are crucial here.
- **Pre-Approval from a Supervisor:** While you *can* apply without a supervisor, finding a faculty member who is willing to support your application before you hit "submit" drastically improves your chances of direct entry at both schools.

## 📝 Application Requirements & Core Documents

1. **Statement of Purpose (SOP):** Needs to be clear about your intended research (Edge AI, Federated Learning) and the specific NUS/NTU faculty you want to work with.
2. **Academic Transcripts & CV.**
3. **Two (2) Academic References.**
4. **English Language Proficiency:** IELTS or TOEFL is strictly required unless you provide official documentation that your degree was taught entirely in English.
5. **The GRE Trap:** 
   - Generally, NUS and NTU have made the GRE **optional**. 
   - **HOWEVER:** If you apply for the SINGA Scholarship (see below) and select a supervisor at **NUS**, they often explicitly require a GRE score. If you select an NTU supervisor, they might be more flexible. If you want to maximize your chances across both, taking the GRE is highly recommended.

# Funding: The SINGA Scholarship & Presidential Awards

Singapore offers incredibly generous, government-backed funding for international students.

## 1. The Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA)
This is a unified, fully funded scholarship for international students pursuing a PhD at A*STAR (the national R&D agency), NUS, or NTU.
- **Full Tuition Fees:** Covered for up to 4 years.
- **Living Stipend:** S$2,700 per month (approx. $2,000 USD).
- **Settling-in Allowance:** A one-time S$1,500 grant.
- **Airfare Grant:** Up to S$1,500 for your flight to Singapore (Unlike the US, they *do* cover your relocation!).

## 2. Presidential Graduate Fellowships (NPGS for NTU / PGF for NUS)
If you apply directly to the universities rather than through SINGA, you are automatically considered for their internal Presidential Fellowships. These offer identical or slightly higher stipends compared to SINGA, plus travel allowances for conferences.

## 📅 Application Process & Timeline (Entry August 2027)

There are two main intakes in Singapore (August and January). For the main **August 2027 intake**:
- **SINGA Scholarship Deadline:** Usually **December 1, 2026**.
- **NUS/NTU Direct Application Deadlines:** Usually ranges from **December 15, 2026 to January 31, 2027** depending on the specific department.

*(Pro Tip: Applying through SINGA allows you to choose research projects at both NUS and NTU simultaneously).*

# 🧑‍🏫 Navigating the Singapore Faculty

Both universities have massive computing faculties with dedicated AI research institutes.

- **At NTU:** Look into the **College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS)**. They have massive labs dedicated to Edge Intelligence and IoT.
- **At NUS:** Look into the **School of Computing**, specifically the Computer Science department's AI and Systems/Networking groups. 

**Next Step:** Review the list of approved SINGA projects (which goes live when applications open) and see if any align with Edge AI. Alternatively, find professors at NTU/NUS and email them your CV and research proposal to ask if they are taking direct-entry PhD students this cycle.
