# Singapore (NUS & NTU): Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The National University of Singapore (NUS) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU) host virtual graduate information sessions, departmental townhalls, and joint briefing webinars with the Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR) regarding the Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA).

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. NUS School of Computing Postgraduate Virtual Information Sessions
- **Dates:** September - October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinars
- **Target Audience:** Prospective PhD and research Master's candidates
- **Description:** Led by the NUS School of Computing Graduate Studies Committee. Covers PhD specializations in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, and Information Systems; direct-entry requirements for bachelor's degree holders; the President's Graduate Fellowship (PGF); and faculty matching strategies.

### 2. NTU College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS) Virtual Briefings
- **Dates:** Late September - October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinars
- **Target Audience:** Prospective research students interested in Edge Intelligence, Cyber-Physical Systems, and Machine Learning
- **Description:** Presentations on NTU CCDS research clusters, laboratory facilities, faculty vacancies, the Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship (NPGS), and supervisor pre-endorsement.

### 3. A*STAR SINGA Scholarship Global Briefing Webinars
- **Dates:** September - November 2026 (Multiple regional sessions)
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Global Webinars
- **Target Audience:** International STEM graduates seeking full doctoral funding in Singapore
- **Description:** Comprehensive overview of the SINGA scheme, covering tuition coverage, monthly stipends, airfare grants, settling-in allowances, and the joint supervision model between A*STAR research institutes and NUS/NTU.

---

## Critical Application Deadlines (August 2027 Intake)
- **Application Portals Open:** August / September 2026
- **SINGA Scholarship Application Deadline:** December 1, 2026 (23:59 Singapore Time)
- **Direct NUS Computing PhD Application Deadline:** Mid-December 2026 (~December 15, 2026)
- **Direct NTU CCDS PhD Application Deadline:** December 2026 - January 2027
- **Outcome Notifications:** March to May 2027
