1. University: Cambridge
Country: United Kingdom (UK)
Scholarship: Gates Cambridge Scholarship (Full tuition, stipend, airfare, visa costs)
Application Opens: September 2026
Application Deadline: Early December 2026 (exact date TBD, usually first week of Dec)
Outcome Notification: Late March 2027

--- EXAMS REQUIRED ---
GRE: NOT required.
English Language (IELTS/TOEFL): REQUIRED (unless you meet specific exemption criteria based on your previous degree). You will likely need to take the IELTS Academic or TOEFL iBT.

--- DOCUMENTS NEEDED ---
1. Statement of Purpose (SOP)
2. Research Proposal
3. Academic Transcripts (from Ahmadu Bello University)
4. Curriculum Vitae (CV)
5. Two (2) Academic References
6. One (1) Personal Reference (Specifically required for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship)
7. Written Work Sample (if requested by the department)
8. Proof of English Proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL scores)

--- APPLICATION PROCESS ---
Is there a separate application letter for the scholarship? 
NO. You apply for both the PhD admission and the Gates Cambridge Scholarship at the exact same time through the Cambridge Graduate Application Portal. There is a specific "funding" section in the main application where you check the box for Gates Cambridge and provide your personal reference details. You do not send a separate letter.

2. University: University of Hong Kong (HKU)
Country: Hong Kong (SAR, China)
Scholarship: Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS) & HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme (HKU-PS). (Covers full tuition, large monthly stipend, conference and travel allowance, and often a first-year cash award).
Application Opens: September 2026
Application Deadline: December 1, 2026 (12:00 noon Hong Kong Time) for the Main Round / HKPFS.
Outcome Notification: Late March to April 2027

--- PROGRAM DETAILS (DIRECT ENTRY) ---
You can apply directly from a Bachelor's degree to the 4-year PhD program. 
Requirement: First-Class Honours and/or a GPA of 3.60+ / 4.00. Because you do not have a Master's degree, strong undergraduate research experience, competition wins (like your Huawei/MATLAB awards), and a very solid research proposal are absolutely critical to stand out.

--- EXAMS REQUIRED ---
GRE: NOT required.
English Language (IELTS/TOEFL): REQUIRED (unless your previous degree was taught entirely in English and you can provide official university proof of this).

--- DOCUMENTS NEEDED ---
1. Statement of Purpose / Vision Statement
2. Detailed Research Proposal
3. Academic Transcripts (showing your 4.53/5.00 CGPA)
4. Curriculum Vitae (CV)
5. Two (2) Academic References
6. Proof of English Proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL)
7. Publications or proof of research experience (e.g., project reports)

--- APPLICATION PROCESS ---
Is there a separate application letter for the scholarship? 
NO separate letter, BUT there is a strict 2-step process for HKPFS:
1. Initial Application: Submit an initial application to the Research Grants Council (RGC) electronic system to obtain an "HKPFS Reference Number".
2. Full Application: Submit your full application on the HKU Graduate School portal and quote your HKPFS Reference Number. By doing this, you are automatically considered for both the HKPFS and HKU's internal Presidential Scholarship.
