## Funding Structure (Employee Model)
Unlike traditional scholarships (like Gates Cambridge), if you are admitted to the Direct Doctorate program at ETH Zurich, you are hired as a university employee. 
- You will receive a standard, highly competitive Swiss salary (often ~70,000 CHF/year).
- This covers living expenses comfortably, and tuition fees are negligible compared to the salary.

## Alternative Funding: Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship (FCS)
As a Nigerian citizen, you are also eligible to apply for the **Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship** for PhD studies. 
- **The Catch:** To apply for this scholarship, you **MUST have an official invitation letter** from an ETH Zurich professor who has already agreed to supervise you. 
- **How it changes your process:** If you want to pursue this route, emailing professors (like the outreach email to Prof. Ana Klimovic) is absolutely mandatory, and you must do it immediately to secure their backing before the November deadline.
- **The Stipend & Mandatory Supplement:** The scholarship pays ~1,920 - 2,450 CHF/month. However, because ETH Zurich PhDs must be paid the standard Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) salary (~70,000 CHF/year), ETH Zurich is **mandated to supplement your scholarship**. They will pay a "differential payment" to bridge the gap between the scholarship and the standard salary.
- **The Strategy:** This makes you incredibly attractive to professors. By winning this scholarship, you bring your own base funding, saving the professor's lab budget tens of thousands of Francs while you still walk away with the full 70,000 CHF total compensation! You should absolutely apply for both.
