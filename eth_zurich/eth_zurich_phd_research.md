# ETH Zurich Direct Doctorate in Computer Science

ETH Zurich (Swiss Federal Institute of Technology) is consistently ranked among the top 5 Computer Science departments globally. The PhD system in Switzerland is entirely different from the US or UK: PhD candidates are considered full-time university employees.

## 🎓 Academic Requirements (Direct Entry)

The standard PhD at ETH Zurich requires a Master's degree. However, they have a highly exclusive **Direct Doctorate in Computer Science** designed specifically for outstanding Bachelor's graduates.

- **The Direct Doctorate Structure:** You effectively complete a highly accelerated Master's degree (often heavily research-focused) before immediately transitioning into the PhD phase.
- **Your Profile:** They are explicitly looking for the top 1-2% of Bachelor's graduates globally. Your 4.53/5.00 First Class CGPA and global awards make you eligible to apply for this competitive track.

## 📝 Application Requirements & Core Documents

1. **Motivation Letter:** This is critical. It must concisely explain your research interests (Edge AI), highlight your Huawei/MATLAB awards, and explicitly mention two specific research groups/professors at ETH Zurich you want to work with.
2. **Academic Transcripts & CV.**
3. **Two (2) Academic References.**
4. **English Language Proficiency:** Required.
5. **GRE:** The GRE is **NOT required** for the Direct Doctorate in Computer Science.

# Funding: The Swiss Employee Model

Because you are treated as a university employee, the funding at ETH Zurich is staggering compared to other countries.

## 💰 What it Covers (Massive Salary)
- **High Salary:** As an employed doctoral student, your salary starts very high (often around CHF 70,000+ per year, which is roughly equivalent to $80,000 USD).
- **Tuition:** Tuition fees are incredibly low in Switzerland and easily covered by your massive salary.
- **Relocation:** While they don't hand you a separate "relocation check" like Cambridge, your initial salary and signing processes provide more than enough liquidity to cover your own flights and moving expenses.

## 📅 Application Process & Timeline (Entry Fall 2027)

Unlike US schools, ETH Zurich's Direct Doctorate has a very strict and slightly earlier application window.

- **Application Window:** Usually **November 1 to November 30, 2026**.
- **Deadline:** November 30 (Strict). You must apply through the "eApply" portal and select "Direct Doctorate".
- **Outcome Notification:** Usually early Spring 2027.
