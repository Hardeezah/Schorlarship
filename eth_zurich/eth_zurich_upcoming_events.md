# ETH Zurich: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
ETH Zurich (Department of Computer Science / D-INFK) offers specialized information sessions covering graduate admissions, direct doctorate tracks, and excellence scholarship funding. While doctoral hiring is frequently conducted directly through individual laboratory professorships, central information sessions provide essential guidance on application windows.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. "Master Your Doctorate" Orientation
- **Date:** September 7, 2026
- **Format:** In-Person / Hybrid Session
- **Target Audience:** Newly entering and prospective doctoral candidates
- **Description:** Comprehensive briefing on doctoral research regulations, department onboarding, academic employment contracts, and doctoral administration at ETH Zurich.

### 2. "Check-In ETH" Graduate Welcome Event
- **Date:** September 11, 2026
- **Format:** Hybrid Event
- **Target Audience:** Incoming graduate students and research staff
- **Description:** Orientation introducing institutional infrastructure, campus resources, computing facilities, and administrative services.

### 3. "Master Your Master" Virtual Information Event
- **Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar
- **Target Audience:** Prospective graduate students and Direct Doctorate applicants
- **Description:** Structured breakdown of the Master's and Direct Doctorate programs in Computer Science (D-INFK). Covers degree specializations, Direct Doctorate eligibility for bachelor's graduates, the Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme (ESOP), and application mechanics.

### 4. Annual Department Open Day (Informatiktage)
- **Date:** March 2027 (Annual Spring Event; March 21 in 2026)
- **Format:** In-Person Workshops, Lectures, and Laboratory Demonstrations
- **Target Audience:** Prospective students and visiting researchers
- **Description:** Annual open house featuring live demonstrations from computer science laboratories, faculty presentations, and research project showcases.
