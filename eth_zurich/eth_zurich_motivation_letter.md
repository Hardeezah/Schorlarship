## Research Experience and the Huawei ICT Innovation Global Win

My core research interest lies in efficient systems for ML and adaptive AI. This focus culminated in my capstone project, *CropDiseaseDetector*, which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, Shenzhen, China). 

The project was born out of a critical infrastructure bottleneck: smallholder farmers in rural Sub-Saharan Africa lack the stable internet connectivity required to transmit gigabytes of crop imagery to cloud servers for disease diagnosis. To solve this, I designed and deployed an agricultural vision model that runs entirely offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. As the sole architect of the system, I successfully compressed and optimized the model to achieve real-time, on-device inference without relying on cloud computation. The full technical report is available via Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22287430), and the open-source implementation is maintained on my GitHub.

While the software model proved robust in controlled tests, deploying it exposed two fundamental challenges that I intend to solve during my PhD:
1. **Systems Efficiency:** Continuous visual inference rapidly exhausts the limited computational resources and energy of low-cost edge devices deployed off-grid. 
2. **Adaptive Reliability:** The model's reliability degrades when encountering unmeasured distribution shifts (e.g., varying lighting conditions or novel crop anomalies) common in the wild.

In addition to my academic research, I am the founding engineer of KodaTrade, a production-level financial platform. Building KodaTrade from the ground up gave me rigorous experience in system architecture, distributed systems, and maintaining highly scalable, production-grade software—skills that perfectly complement the engineering demands of advanced systems research.

## Proposed Research Direction at ETH Zurich

ETH Zurich is my top choice for doctoral studies because of its unparalleled excellence in both systems engineering and theoretical machine learning. I am specifically interested in conducting research under the supervision of **Prof. Ana Klimovic** and **Prof. Andreas Krause**, as my proposed research bridges their respective domains.

My primary research objective is to develop ultra-low-power, adaptive computer vision systems for extreme edge environments. 

Under the guidance of **Prof. Ana Klimovic**, I aim to investigate how computer systems for AI can be aggressively optimized to support continuous visual inference on resource-constrained devices. Her group’s pioneering work in building efficient infrastructure for machine learning is exactly the framework required to push models like CropDiseaseDetector beyond current computational limits. I want to explore novel systems-level optimizations tailored specifically for embedded architectures deployed in off-grid agricultural settings.

Simultaneously, deploying models in remote environments inherently involves encountering out-of-distribution data. Drawing on the expertise of **Prof. Andreas Krause's Learning and Adaptive Systems Group**, I aim to integrate adaptive machine learning techniques into these efficient systems frameworks. His work on reliable AI and adaptive systems in uncertain environments provides the theoretical foundation necessary to ensure that edge models do not fail silently when faced with severe distribution shifts in the wild.
