# ETH Zurich PhD Outreach Emails

## 1. Email to Prof. Ana Klimovic
**Focus:** Computer Systems for AI, Efficient ML Infrastructure, and Resource-Constrained Environments

**Subject:** Prospective Direct Doctorate Student (2027) – Systems for ML Optimization in Constrained Agricultural Vision

Dear Prof. Klimovic,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining your research group for the Direct Doctorate program in Computer Science (D-INFK) at ETH Zurich. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My engineering background focuses on building and deploying ML systems under severe hardware and energy constraints, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed entirely offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. While the software model itself is robust, the critical bottleneck in deploying this to rural smallholder farmers is system efficiency. We need systems for ML that can perform real-time, on-device inference without rapidly draining the computational resources and batteries of low-cost edge devices.

I have been closely following your extensive work on Computer Systems for AI and energy-efficient AI infrastructure. Your focus on building efficient systems for machine learning is exactly the dimension I want to explore for my PhD. I am proposing to investigate how we can further optimize computer vision inference systems specifically for ultra-low-power, resource-constrained embedded environments deployed in extreme, off-grid agricultural settings.

Given my practical experience deploying offline models and my strong background in systems and algorithmic optimization (recognized by my national MATLAB awards), I am deeply interested in the efficient ML infrastructure challenges your group tackles. My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App).

Are you accepting new students for the upcoming cycle, and would investigating systems for ML in extreme resource-constrained environments align with your current research focus?

Thank you for your time,
Hadiza Mohammed
