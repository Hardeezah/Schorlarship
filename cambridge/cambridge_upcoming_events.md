# University of Cambridge: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The University of Cambridge organizes postgraduate admissions events through central webinars and the annual Virtual Postgraduate Open Days. These sessions offer direct access to faculty, current students, funding advisors, and admissions officers.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. Warm-Up Webinar: Choosing and Applying for a Master's or PhD
- **Date:** September 7, 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar
- **Target Audience:** Prospective postgraduate applicants (Master's and PhD)
- **Description:** Guidance on finding the right research degree, preparing application documents, writing a research proposal, and navigating the applicant portal.

### 2. Cambridge College Visit Days
- **Dates:** September 17 - 21, 2026
- **Format:** In-Person Campus Visits
- **Target Audience:** Prospective graduate students
- **Description:** On-campus visits to explore individual Cambridge Colleges, college libraries, accommodation, and research communities.

### 3. Warm-Up Webinar: Postgraduate Finance and Funding
- **Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar
- **Target Audience:** International and domestic applicants seeking full funding
- **Description:** Dedicated overview of major funding bodies, including the Gates Cambridge Scholarship, Cambridge Trust, department studentships, and deadline requirements.

### 4. Virtual Postgraduate Open Days (Flagship Event)
- **Dates:** November 2 - 13, 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Platform (Live webinars, recorded presentations, and interactive Q&A sessions)
- **Structure:**
  - **Week 1 (November 2 - 6, 2026): Academic Departments & Subject Sessions**
    - Subject-specific sessions for the Department of Computer Science and Technology.
    - Research group overviews, lab tours, and faculty Q&A regarding direct-from-bachelor's PhD admissions.
  - **Week 2 (November 9 - 13, 2026): Cambridge Colleges and Support Services**
    - College admissions sessions, funding workshops, and student support presentations.

## Critical Application Deadlines
- **Graduate Application Portal Opens:** September 2026
- **Gates Cambridge Scholarship Deadline (International Applicants):** Early December 2026
- **Cambridge Trust & Department Funding Deadline:** Early December 2026 / Early January 2027 (course specific)
- **Outcome Notifications:** Late March 2027
