# Cambridge PhD Application Details

## Program & Scholarship Information
- **University:** University of Cambridge
- **Program:** PhD in Computer Science (Direct from Bachelor's route)
- **Primary Scholarship Target:** The Gates Cambridge Scholarship
- **Funding Coverage:** Full University Composition Fee (tuition), maintenance allowance (living stipend ~£21,000–£22,050), one economy single airfare at both the beginning and end of the course, and inbound visa costs (including the Immigration Health Surcharge).

## Important Dates & Timeline (Entry 2027/28)
*Assuming application from Nigeria (International Round)*

- **September 2026:** Graduate Application Portal opens.
- **Mid-October 2026:** Start contacting potential supervisors (Prof. Nic Lane, Dr. Ferenc Huszár) to discuss your research proposal if you haven't already.
- **Early December 2026 / Early January 2027:** Typical deadline for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship and the PhD program for international applicants. *(Note: You must check the exact date on the Cambridge Course Directory when the 2027 portal opens, as it varies slightly each year. It is usually the first week of December).*
- **Late March 2027:** Gates Cambridge Scholarship outcome notification.

## Required Application Documents

### 1. Statement of Purpose (SOP)
- Your updated SOP detailing your research interests, alignment with Cambridge faculty (Nic Lane, Ferenc Huszár), and your "commitment to improving the lives of others" (crucial for Gates Cambridge).

### 2. Research Proposal
- A detailed, formal research proposal. Given your direct-from-bachelor's route, this must be exceptionally strong. It should ideally be drafted with some input or alignment from your prospective supervisor.

### 3. Academic Transcripts & Certificates
- Official transcripts from Ahmadu Bello University showing your First Class Honours and 4.53/5.00 CGPA.

### 4. Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Highlighting your founding engineer roles, Huawei ICT Innovation Competition global win, MATLAB Student Coding Challenge win, and UNITAR forum presentation.

### 5. Two (2) Academic References
- Letters of recommendation. Given your background, Dr. Zaharuddeen Haruna and Dr. Salisu Aliyu are ideal choices. They need to speak to your academic rigor and readiness to jump straight into a PhD.

### 6. Personal Reference (For Gates Cambridge Only)
- The Gates Cambridge scholarship requires a *third* reference specifically addressing your fit for the scholarship (your leadership potential and commitment to improving the lives of others).

### 7. Proof of English Language Proficiency
- If required, you will need to submit valid IELTS or TOEFL scores meeting the department's stringent requirements.

### 8. Written Work Sample
- The Computer Science department or your specific prospective supervisor may ask for a sample of your written academic work.
