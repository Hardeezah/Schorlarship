# University of Washington (UW): Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington conducts virtual prospective PhD student information sessions and Q&A panels during autumn. Because the Allen School admits direct-to-PhD candidates by default, these sessions focus on research potential, lab selection, Statement of Purpose writing, and fully funded graduate research appointments.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. Paul G. Allen School PhD Admissions Virtual Information Sessions
- **Dates:** October - Early November 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Zoom Webinars
- **Target Audience:** Prospective PhD applicants in Computer Science and Engineering
- **Description:** Led by the Allen School graduate admissions faculty and graduate student ambassadors. The session covers holistic application review, direct-entry admissions for bachelor's degree holders, active research clusters (Machine Learning, Computer Systems, Ubiquitous Computing), and what faculty look for in a Statement of Purpose.

### 2. UW Central Graduate School Virtual Information Fair
- **Dates:** Mid-October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Fair
- **Target Audience:** Prospective graduate students across all disciplines
- **Description:** Central sessions covering university-wide admission procedures, international student services, visa documentation, and campus housing.

---

## Critical Application Deadlines (Autumn 2027 Intake)
- **Graduate Application System Opens:** September 2026
- **Paul G. Allen School Application Deadline:** December 15, 2026 (Strict deadline; late materials not reviewed)
  - Requires 3 letters of recommendation, CV, SOP, and official transcripts. GRE scores are explicitly not accepted.
- **Outcome Notifications:** February to March 2027
