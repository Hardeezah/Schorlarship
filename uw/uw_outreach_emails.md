# University of Washington PhD Outreach Emails

## 1. Email to Prof. Pang Wei Koh (Paul G. Allen School)
**Focus:** Domain Generalization and Reliability of AI Systems in the Real World

**Subject:** Prospective PhD Student (2027) – Domain Generalization for Constrained Agricultural Vision

Dear Prof. Koh,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining your research group at the Paul G. Allen School as a PhD student. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My engineering background focuses on building ML systems for unpredictable, real-world environments, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. While the model achieves high accuracy in controlled tests, deploying it to smallholder farmers exposed a critical vulnerability: the model's reliability drops significantly when confronted with unmeasured distribution shifts (e.g., varying smartphone camera sensors, novel lighting conditions, or degraded crop appearances in new geographic domains).

I have been closely following your research on domain generalization and making AI systems more reliable in the wild. Your work on leveraging domain relations and understanding how models extrapolate beyond their training distributions is highly relevant to my field challenges. For my PhD, I want to investigate how these theoretical domain generalization frameworks can be applied to and measured in constrained visual edge environments, ensuring that agricultural vision models do not fail silently when encountering out-of-distribution data.

Given my practical experience deploying offline models and my strong mathematical background (recognized by my national MATLAB awards), I am deeply interested in the robust ML challenges your group tackles. My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App).

Are you planning to take on new PhD students for the upcoming cycle, and would investigating domain generalization for extreme visual environments align with your current research focus?

Thank you for your time,
Hadiza Mohammed
