# University of Washington (Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science)

The University of Washington (UW) in Seattle is consistently ranked in the global top 10 for Computer Science. Because it is located in the backyard of Amazon and Microsoft headquarters, their Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science is a global powerhouse for Systems, Machine Learning, and Edge Computing.

## 🎓 Academic Requirements & Direct Entry

In the US system, the PhD is a 5-year track that includes a Master's degree built-in.
- **Direct Entry:** The Allen School explicitly recruits students directly from a Bachelor's degree. In fact, they do not even offer a standalone Master's degree for full-time students; you apply directly to the PhD.
- **Your Profile:** Your 4.53/5.00 CGPA, industrial background, and Huawei awards make you highly competitive. Because they emphasize research potential over test scores, your project history is your strongest asset.

## 📝 Application Requirements & Core Documents

1. **Statement of Purpose:** Needs to be highly tailored, showing why you belong in the Allen School and which professors you want to work with.
2. **Three (3) Academic References.**
3. **Academic Transcripts & CV.**
4. **English Language Proficiency:** TOEFL or IELTS is strictly required for international students (unless you have a waiver).
5. **GRE:** The GRE is **NOT accepted**. They explicitly state they will not look at GRE scores even if you submit them. This is a huge advantage for you!

# Funding: The Fully Funded Guarantee

Like all top-tier US Computer Science PhD programs, the Allen School is fully funded by default.

## 💰 What it Covers
- **Full Tuition:** 100% of your tuition is covered.
- **Living Stipend:** A highly competitive monthly living stipend (usually around $3,500 - $4,000+ USD per month depending on TA/RA appointments).
- **Health Insurance:** Full coverage.
- *(Note: Remember, like other US schools, they do not provide a "relocation check" or buy your flights upfront. You use your stipend to cover living expenses).*

## 📅 Application Process & Timeline (Entry Fall 2027)

- **Application Deadline:** **December 15, 2026**. (This is a hard deadline).
- **Outcome Notification:** Usually February to March 2027.
- **Separate Scholarship App?** NO. All admitted PhD students are automatically guaranteed the full funding package.
