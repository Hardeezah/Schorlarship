# University of Hong Kong (HKU) PhD in Computer Science: Direct from Bachelor's Route

It is entirely possible to apply for a PhD at the University of Hong Kong (HKU) directly from a Bachelor's degree. In fact, HKU has a specific track designed for this.

## 🎓 Academic Requirements (The 4-Year PhD)

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> **The 4-Year vs. 3-Year Track**  
> Students applying *without* a research Master's degree (like an MPhil) are admitted to the **4-year PhD track**. This extra year provides time for required coursework and a probationary period to get you up to speed on rigorous academic research.

- **Degree Standard:** You must hold a **first-class honours degree**.
- **GPA Requirement:** For the Department of Computer Science, a CGPA of **3.60 or higher out of 4.00** is highly recommended for direct entry. With your 4.53/5.00, you are well above this academic threshold.
- **Research Potential:** Because you do not have a Master's degree to prove your research chops, the admissions committee will look heavily at your undergraduate projects, competition wins (Huawei, MATLAB), and any publications you might have. Your **Research Proposal** is the single most critical document in your application.

## 📝 Application Requirements & Core Documents

1. **Research Proposal:** A highly detailed and well-structured proposal showing you understand the current state of the art in your field (e.g., Federated Learning on Edge Devices) and have a clear plan for your 4 years.
2. **Statement of Purpose / Vision Statement:** Explaining your background and why HKU.
3. **Academic Transcripts:** Showing your First-Class Honours.
4. **Comprehensive CV:** Highlighting your founding engineer roles and global competition wins.
5. **Two Academic References.**
6. **English Language Proficiency:** You must submit valid IELTS or TOEFL scores *unless* you can provide official documentation from Ahmadu Bello University explicitly stating that your entire degree was taught in English.
7. **GRE:** The GRE is **NOT required**.

# Funding: The Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS)

The HKPFS is the most prestigious government-backed PhD scholarship in Hong Kong. It is highly competitive and designed to attract top international talent.

## 💰 What it Covers
- **Annual Stipend:** A massive monthly stipend (approximately HK$336,600 / ~US$43,100 per year) for up to 3 years (with the university often covering the 4th year).
- **Conference and Travel Allowance:** Around HK$13,800 (~US$1,760) per year for research-related travel.
- **HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme (HKU-PS):** If you apply for the HKPFS, you are automatically considered for the HKU-PS, which offers additional cash awards in your first year (up to HK$40,000 / ~US$5,100) and waivers for the full tuition fee!

## 📅 Application Process & Strict Timeline (Entry 2027)

> [!WARNING]  
> **The Strict 2-Step Process**  
> Unlike Cambridge, there is a strict two-stage process you must follow to get the HKPFS scholarship. 

1. **Step 1: Initial Application (RGC).** You must submit an initial application through the Hong Kong Research Grants Council (RGC) electronic system. This opens in September 2026. You will receive an **HKPFS Reference Number**. 
   - **Deadline:** December 1, 2026 (12:00 noon Hong Kong Time).
2. **Step 2: Full Application (HKU).** You must then submit your full PhD application on the HKU Graduate School portal and quote that exact HKPFS Reference Number.

# 🧑‍🏫 Navigating the HKU Faculty

Just like Cambridge, you need to find a supervisor whose work aligns with your goals (Edge AI, Federated Learning, embedded devices).

At HKU, this research is primarily done across the **Department of Computer Science** and the **Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE)**.

**What to look for:**
Look for faculty working on **"Federated Split Learning (FSL)"** or **"Edge Intelligence."** HKU researchers have been publishing heavily on how to overcome the memory and compute limitations of edge devices (like your Orange Pi) by splitting models between the device and edge servers, as well as tackling "Byzantine-robust" federated learning (dealing with adversarial clients in decentralized networks).

**Next Step:** You should browse the HKU Computer Science faculty directory, find 2-3 professors working in "Edge Intelligence" or "Federated Learning," read a recent paper of theirs, and reach out to them via email with your CV and research proposal draft *before* you apply.
