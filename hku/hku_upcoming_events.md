# University of Hong Kong (HKU): Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The University of Hong Kong Graduate School and Faculty of Engineering hold international webinars and briefing sessions throughout autumn. These sessions focus on the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS), the HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme (HKU-PS), and the 4-year direct-entry PhD track from a bachelor's degree.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. HKU Graduate School HKPFS Virtual Information Sessions
- **Dates:** September - November 2026 (Multiple rolling sessions scheduled across autumn)
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar Series
- **Target Audience:** International direct-entry PhD candidates and research master's graduates
- **Description:** Detailed presentation by the HKU Graduate School covering the HKPFS fellowship package (tuition waiver, monthly stipend, conference travel grants, and cash awards), the two-step application process (RGC + HKU Portal), selection criteria, and research proposal standards.

### 2. Faculty of Engineering / Department of Computer Science Research Webinars
- **Dates:** October - November 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Q&A
- **Target Audience:** Prospective PhD students in Computer Science and Edge AI
- **Description:** Presentations by engineering faculty showcasing active research areas, departmental labs, and supervisor collaboration expectations for direct-entry applicants.
