**Focus:** Operational AI and Edge Inference for Computer Vision

**Subject:** Prospective PhD Student (2027) – Operational Edge AI for Constrained Vision Environments

Dear Prof. Wong,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining the Vision and Image Processing (VIP) Lab as a PhD student. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My engineering background focuses on building operational computer vision systems for unpredictable, severely constrained environments, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed entirely offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. While the software model itself is robust, the critical bottleneck in scaling this to rural smallholder farmers is hardware efficiency—specifically, achieving high-accuracy, real-time inference on low-cost, off-grid edge devices without rapid battery drain. 

I have been closely following your extensive work in operational AI and edge intelligence. Your approach to designing highly optimized, compact machine learning models that can actually be deployed in real-world edge environments is exactly the dimension I want to explore for my PhD. I am proposing to investigate novel architectural optimizations and compression techniques for computer vision inference, specifically tailored for ultra-low-power embedded systems deployed in extreme agricultural environments.

Given my practical experience deploying offline models and my strong background in mathematics and algorithmic optimization (recognized by my national MATLAB awards), I am deeply interested in the operational AI challenges your lab tackles. My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App).
