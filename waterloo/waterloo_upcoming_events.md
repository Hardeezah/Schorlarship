# University of Waterloo: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The University of Waterloo Graduate Studies and Postdoctoral Affairs (GSPA) and the David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science organize annual virtual information sessions and webinars throughout the autumn term. These events provide prospective graduate researchers with guidance on direct-entry PhD pathways, supervisor matching, and guaranteed base funding.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. "Discover Graduate Studies at Waterloo" Virtual Series
- **Dates:** September 23 - 25, 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar Series
- **Target Audience:** Prospective Master's and Doctoral applicants
- **Description:** Central university information sessions hosted by GSPA. Topics include graduate admission prerequisites, writing personal statements, preparing academic references, understanding minimum funding guarantees, and work permit pathways in Canada.

### 2. Cheriton School of Computer Science Graduate Webinars
- **Dates:** October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Interactive Sessions
- **Target Audience:** Prospective Computer Science PhD and MMath candidates
- **Description:** Departmental presentations focusing on computer science research groups, systems and artificial intelligence research, direct-entry PhD criteria from bachelor's degrees, and how to effectively initiate contact with prospective research supervisors.

---

## Critical Application Deadlines (Fall 2027 Intake)
- **Application Portal Opens (OUAC / Waterloo):** September 2026
- **Computer Science PhD Application Deadline:** December 15, 2026
  - Requires 3 academic references, transcripts, statement of purpose, and CV.
- **Outcome Notifications:** Late February to April 2027
