# Stanford University: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The Stanford University Computer Science Department and the School of Engineering hold virtual graduate information panels and prospective student Q&A sessions each autumn. These sessions highlight direct-entry PhD applications, Statement of Purpose expectations, research fit with Stanford AI and Systems labs, and fully funded fellowship packages.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. Stanford Computer Science PhD Admissions Virtual Information Sessions
- **Dates:** Mid-October - Early November 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar & Interactive Student Panel
- **Target Audience:** Prospective PhD applicants in Computer Science
- **Description:** Led by members of the CS Admissions Committee and current doctoral students. The panel details the holistic review process, evaluating applicants directly from undergraduate degrees without a master's, crafting a research-centered Statement of Purpose, and identifying prospective faculty mentors.

### 2. Stanford School of Engineering / EDGE Virtual Sessions
- **Dates:** October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar
- **Target Audience:** Prospective graduate engineering candidates
- **Description:** Broad overview of engineering research facilities, interdisciplinary collaborations, graduate life in the Bay Area, and fellowship funding models.

---

## Critical Application Deadlines (Autumn 2027 Intake)
- **Graduate Application System Opens:** Late September 2026
- **Stanford Computer Science PhD Application Deadline:** Early December 2026 (Typically December 1 - December 6, 2026)
  - All letters of recommendation (3 required), transcripts, and application materials must be completed by the deadline.
- **Outcome Notifications:** February to March 2027
