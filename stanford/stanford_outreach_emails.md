# Stanford PhD Outreach Emails

## 1. Email to Prof. Sanmi Koyejo (STAIR Lab)
**Focus:** Asynchronous Federated Optimization for Edge Devices in Low-Bandwidth Environments

**Subject:** Prospective PhD Student (2027) – Asynchronous Federated Edge Learning for Agriculture

Dear Prof. Koyejo,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining the Stanford Trustworthy AI Research (STAIR) lab as a PhD student. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My applied ML engineering focuses on deploying robust models under severe hardware and network constraints, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed entirely offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. Continuously improving it with real-world field data across smallholder farmers presents a structural bottleneck: we cannot reliably transmit gigabytes of crop imagery from rural farms over poor 2G/3G networks to a central server.

I read your paper on "Asynchronous Federated Optimization" and was deeply impressed by the algorithmic approach to improving flexibility and scalability in training across massive numbers of highly heterogeneous edge devices. This asynchronous approach directly mirrors the constraint I face with CropDiseaseDetector: devices coming online intermittently on unstable rural connections. I am proposing a PhD research direction that builds on this asynchronous federated optimization, explicitly investigating how it can be adapted for constrained, image-based inference in agricultural deployments, ensuring models are updated reliably without moving raw field data.

Given my practical experience deploying offline models, I am deeply interested in tackling the systems-level bottlenecks of trustworthy edge AI that the STAIR lab focuses on. My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App).

Are you planning to take on new PhD students for the upcoming cycle, and would this applied direction align with your current lab focus?

Thank you for your time,
Hadiza Mohammed
