# Stanford University Direct PhD Application Checklist

This document tracks all required materials, deadlines, and funding details for the Stanford University Computer Science PhD program.

## Important Dates
- **Application Portal Opens:** September 2026
- **Application Deadline:** Early December 2026 (for Fall 2027 intake)
- **Outcome Notification:** February to March 2027

## Funding Structure (Fully Funded by Default)
At Stanford, the Computer Science PhD program is **fully funded by default** for all admitted students. 
- **The Process:** You do NOT need to apply for a separate scholarship. If the admissions committee accepts you into the PhD program, you are automatically given a comprehensive funding package.
- **What is covered:** This package covers your **full tuition**, **comprehensive health insurance**, and provides a generous **living stipend** (usually through a mix of fellowships, Research Assistantships, or Teaching Assistantships).

- [ ] **Academic Transcripts & Degree Certificate**
  - *Requirement:* Official transcripts showing your First Class Honours from Ahmadu Bello University. You CAN apply directly with a Bachelor's degree; a Master's is not required.
- [ ] **Three (3) Letters of Recommendation**
  - *Requirement:* Standard for US universities. You have 5 recommenders confirmed; select your top 3 for Stanford. They will need to submit these online.
- [ ] **Proof of English Proficiency**
  - *Requirement:* Valid IELTS or TOEFL scores. (Check Stanford's portal to see if your English-taught degree from Nigeria exempts you).
- [ ] **GRE Scores**
  - *Requirement:* NOT required for Stanford Computer Science.
