# University of Oxford: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
Graduate open days at the University of Oxford are organized primarily at the academic department level. The Department of Computer Science hosts dedicated online graduate open days and information sessions for prospective DPhil (PhD) candidates, covering supervisor contact, research proposals, and Clarendon scholarship nomination.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. Oxford Internet Institute (OII) Online Information Sessions
- **Dates:** September 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar
- **Target Audience:** Prospective DPhil and MSc applicants in interdisciplinary computational fields
- **Description:** Sessions covering computational social science, AI governance, and interdisciplinary data research programs at Oxford.

### 2. Department of Computer Science Online Graduate Open Day (Flagship Event)
- **Date:** Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (1:00 PM - 3:00 PM UK Time)
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Session
- **Target Audience:** Prospective DPhil in Computer Science and MSc in Advanced Computer Science applicants
- **Description:** Detailed presentation by departmental admissions tutors covering course structure, research themes (Machine Learning, Automated Verification, Algorithms, Edge Computing), supervisor selection, the EPSRC CDT in Autonomous Intelligent Machines and Systems (AIMS), and the application review process. Includes an open Q&A session with faculty and current graduate researchers.

### 3. Department of Materials / Interdisciplinary Science Open Days
- **Date:** October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Event
- **Target Audience:** Prospective research students interested in hardware, sensors, and nanoscale devices

---

## Critical Application Deadlines
- **Graduate Application Portal Opens:** September 2026
- **DPhil in Computer Science Application Deadline (Major Funding Round):** Early December 2026 (First week of December)
  - Submitting before this deadline guarantees automatic consideration for the Clarendon Fund, EPSRC CDT studentships, and college-backed awards.
- **Outcome Notifications:** Late February to March 2027
