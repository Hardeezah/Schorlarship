# University of Oxford: DPhil in Computer Science & AIMS CDT

Oxford is a global titan in Artificial Intelligence. For someone with your profile (First-Class Honours, Edge AI focus), Oxford offers two distinct but highly overlapping pathways for a fully funded PhD (which they call a DPhil).

## 🎓 The EPSRC CDT in Autonomous Intelligent Machines and Systems (AIMS)

If your goal is to work on Edge AI, embedded machine learning, and systems, the **AIMS CDT** is arguably the best program in Europe. 

- **What is a CDT?** A Centre for Doctoral Training (CDT) is a massive, heavily funded UK initiative. Instead of jumping straight into a 3-year solo research project, the CDT is a structured **4-year program**.
- **Direct Entry (The 1+3 Model):** The AIMS CDT is explicitly designed to take students directly from a Bachelor's degree. Your first year consists of advanced coursework and "mini-projects" across different labs. The next 3 years are spent purely on your DPhil research. 
- **Requirement:** A First-Class Honours degree. Your 4.53/5.00 easily qualifies you. Your industrial engineering background and Huawei/MATLAB awards will make your application stand out heavily in the competitive admissions pool.

*(Note: You can also apply for the standard 3-year DPhil in Computer Science, but the AIMS CDT is generally much better funded and structured for Bachelor's graduates).*

## 📝 Application Requirements & Core Documents

1. **Research Proposal:** A strong, unique research proposal is required. For the CDT, you should outline the broad area of Edge AI / Federated Learning you wish to tackle.
2. **Pre-Approval (Recommended):** While the CDT structure means you don't *strictly* need a supervisor on day one (you finalize this after year 1), it is still highly recommended to contact Oxford faculty beforehand to ensure your interests align with their current grants.
3. **Three (3) Academic References.**
4. **Academic Transcripts & CV.**
5. **English Language Proficiency:** Strictly required (IELTS/TOEFL) unless your degree was taught entirely in English and you secure a waiver.
6. **GRE:** The GRE is **NOT required** for Oxford.

# Funding: The Clarendon Scholarship & CDT Funding

Oxford is notoriously expensive, but you have two massive funding avenues that trigger automatically.

## 1. The Clarendon Scholarship
The Clarendon Fund is Oxford’s flagship scholarship (the equivalent of Cambridge's Gates Scholarship).
- **What it covers:** 100% of your tuition fees and a generous annual living grant (usually around £18,622 to £20,000+ tax-free).
- **Application Process:** There is **NO separate application**. If you submit your DPhil application by the early December deadline, you are automatically considered for the Clarendon Scholarship based purely on academic merit.

## 2. Direct CDT Funding
Because the AIMS CDT is backed by the UK government (EPSRC) and industry partners (like DeepMind, Amazon, and Meta), the program itself comes with massive internal funding.
- International students admitted to the CDT are often offered full funding packages directly from the department/industry sponsors if they miss out on the Clarendon.
- **Relocation:** While the base Oxford funding packages do not explicitly hand out a "flight check" like the Gates Cambridge, the AIMS CDT has massive internal travel budgets. Students are frequently given thousands of pounds to travel to global conferences or relocate for industry internships.

## 📅 Application Process & Timeline (Entry Fall 2027)

- **Application Opens:** September 2026.
- **Application Deadline:** Usually **early December 2026** (e.g., Dec 1 or Dec 3). *You MUST meet this deadline to be considered for the Clarendon Scholarship and the primary CDT funding.*
- **Outcome Notification:** Interviews usually happen in late January, with offers in February/March.
