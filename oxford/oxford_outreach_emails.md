# Oxford PhD Outreach Emails

## 1. Email to Prof. Yarin Gal (OATML Group)
**Focus:** Out-of-Distribution Generalization and Uncertainty in Real-World Constrained Environments

**Subject:** Prospective DPhil Student (2027) – OOD Generalization in Constrained Visual Environments

Dear Prof. Gal,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining the Oxford Applied and Theoretical Machine Learning (OATML) group for my DPhil. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My engineering background focuses on building ML systems for unpredictable environments, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. However, in deploying this toward real smallholder farmers, I recognized a critical gap: measuring model reliability under extreme real-world distribution shifts—such as different camera lenses in the field or varying lighting conditions. 

I recently read your paper, "What 'Out-of-distribution' Is and Is Not," and appreciated the rigorous disambiguation of OOD data types. This framework is highly relevant to my field challenges. When a farmer from a new region submits crop data, determining whether it represents a "transformed-distribution" (e.g., new lighting) or a "related-distribution" (e.g., a novel crop disease mutation) is critical for system reliability. 

For my DPhil, I want to investigate how these rigorous OOD definitions and uncertainty quantification methods can be practically integrated into constrained visual edge environments, ensuring models deployed in the wild don't just fail silently when encountering novel distribution shifts.

My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App). 

Are you accepting new students for the upcoming cycle, and does bridging your theoretical OOD work with applied visual edge environments align with the current interests of the OATML group?

Thank you for your time,
Hadiza Mohammed
