## 1. Email to Prof. Niki Trigoni [DRAFT - Ready to Send]
**Role:** Director, EPSRC AIMS CDT & Head of Cyber Physical Systems Group  
**Recipient:** `niki.trigoni@cs.ox.ac.uk`  

**Subject:** Prospective DPhil Applicant (Rhodes & Clarendon 2027): Energy-Efficient Embedded AI – Hadiza Mohammed

Dear Prof. Trigoni,

My name is Hadiza Mohammed. I graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University, Nigeria (CGPA 4.53/5.00), and won First Prize globally at the Huawei AI Innovation Challenge Finals (2025).

For that competition, I architected CropDiseaseDetector, an autonomous agricultural computer vision device deployed offline on an Orange Pi AI Pro board (ARM architecture with an Ascend 310B NPU). By implementing INT8 quantization, zero-copy buffer sharing, and memory-pipelined multi-task scheduling, we eliminated thermal throttling and sustained real-time offline inference under sub-watt constraints. The project repository is available at: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron

I have been following your research at the Cyber Physical Systems Group and your leadership of the AIMS CDT at Oxford. Your focus on designing intelligent sensor systems that are energy-efficient and resilient in real-world physical environments directly connects with the systems bottlenecks I tackled in off-grid agricultural deployment.

For my DPhil research, I want to investigate energy-efficient edge AI runtimes and low-power hardware-software co-design for autonomous cyber-physical systems, focusing on optimizing multi-task neural network execution on constrained embedded hardware without cloud dependency.

I am preparing my applications for the Rhodes Scholarship for West Africa (September deadline) and the Oxford DPhil in Computer Science / AIMS CDT for Autumn 2027 entry (December deadline). Would you be open to supervising this research direction, and are you accepting new DPhil students for the upcoming cycle?

I have attached my academic CV and undergraduate transcript for your review.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
