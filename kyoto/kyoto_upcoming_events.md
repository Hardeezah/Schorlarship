# Kyoto University: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
Admissions for international doctoral research candidates at Kyoto University Graduate School of Informatics are organized around the MEXT Scholarship pathways (Embassy Recommendation vs. University Recommendation) and the preliminary Admissions Assistance Office (AAO) clearance process.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. Graduate School of Informatics Entrance Examination Briefings (Nyushi Setsumeikai)
- **Schedule:** Held annually in Spring (May; specific dates announced each April)
- **Format:** In-Person / Hybrid Webinar Sessions
- **Target Audience:** Prospective Master's and Doctoral applicants
- **Description:** Department-specific briefings covering course structures, laboratory specializations (Intelligence Science and Technology, Social Informatics, Communications and Computer Engineering), entrance exam guidelines, and research student enrollment.

### 2. Admissions Assistance Office (AAO) Clearance Process
- **Schedule:** Active throughout autumn for 2027 admissions
- **Format:** Online Portal Assessment
- **Target Audience:** All international graduates from non-Japanese institutions
- **Description:** Mandatory preliminary screening where candidate degrees, transcripts, and research plans are evaluated by the Kyoto University AAO before any formal professor contact or application submission can proceed.

---

## Critical Application Deadlines and Milestones
- **September 1, 2026:** Strict deadline for requesting a Letter of Provisional Acceptance from Kyoto University professors for Embassy-recommended MEXT candidates.
- **November - December 2026:** Professor outreach and laboratory selection window for the University Recommendation MEXT Scholarship track (for October 2027 matriculation).
- **January - February 2027:** Official submission of University Recommendation MEXT application dossiers to the Japanese Ministry of Education (MEXT).
- **June - July 2027:** Final MEXT scholarship award announcements.
