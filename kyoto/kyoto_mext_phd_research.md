# Kyoto University (via MEXT Scholarship)

Kyoto University is Japan's second-oldest university and one of the highest-ranked institutions in Asia. Their Graduate School of Informatics is a global leader in computing. However, the Japanese academic system and the MEXT scholarship process are highly unique.

## 🎓 Academic Requirements & The MEXT Pathway

In Japan, it is highly unusual to skip the Master's degree entirely. The standard path is a 2-year Master's followed by a 3-year PhD. 

- **The Pathway:** Because you only have a Bachelor's degree, you will likely need to apply for the MEXT scholarship to cover a **5-year integrated track** (starting as a "Research Student", entering the Master's program, and seamlessly transitioning into the PhD).
- **Admissions Assistance Office (AAO):** Before you can even contact a professor at Kyoto University, international applicants usually must pass a preliminary review by the university's AAO to verify their foreign degree equivalence.
- **Your Profile:** Your First-Class degree and Huawei awards are exactly what the highly competitive MEXT screening committees look for.

## 📝 Application Requirements & Core Documents

1. **Research Plan:** MEXT requires a very detailed, formal research plan.
2. **Pre-Approval (Mandatory):** You absolutely must secure a "Letter of Provisional Acceptance" from a Kyoto University professor.
3. **Academic Transcripts & CV.**
4. **Letter of Recommendation:** Usually from your University Dean or academic advisor.
5. **English Language Proficiency:** TOEFL or IELTS is required if you are applying for an English-taught program.
6. **Japanese Language:** Not strictly required for the CS/Informatics PhD (as research is in English), but MEXT provides 6 months of intensive Japanese language training upon arrival.
7. **GRE:** Not required.

# Funding: The MEXT Scholarship (Japanese Government)

The MEXT (Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology) scholarship is the holy grail of funding in Japan. It is fully funded and incredibly prestigious.

## 💰 What it Covers (Everything)
- **Full Tuition:** 100% of entrance examination, matriculation, and tuition fees at Kyoto University.
- **Living Stipend:** A monthly stipend of approximately 143,000 to 145,000 JPY (this is very comfortable for a student in Kyoto).
- **Relocation & Travel:** MEXT completely covers your **round-trip airfare** (providing a ticket from Nigeria to Japan at the start of your studies, and a return ticket when you graduate).

## 📅 Application Process & Timeline

There are two ways to get the MEXT scholarship:
1. **Embassy Recommendation:** You apply through the Japanese Embassy in Nigeria. The primary screening (exams and interviews) happens around **May/June 2026** for a Fall 2027 start. If you pass, you then contact Kyoto University for a Letter of Acceptance.
2. **University Recommendation:** You apply directly to a professor at Kyoto University, and they nominate you to MEXT. Deadlines for this are usually around **December 2026 to January 2027**.
