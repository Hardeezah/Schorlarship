# Imperial College London: Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
Imperial College London hosts postgraduate virtual open days, department information sessions, and international recruitment webinars throughout the autumn term. These events provide guidance on the President's PhD Scholarships, research proposal requirements, and securing supervisor pre-approval.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. Virtual Open Day: MRes Experimental Biomolecular Sciences
- **Date:** September 11, 2026 (13:30 - 14:30 BST)
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Session
- **Target Audience:** Prospective research graduate students
- **Description:** Department overview, research laboratory presentations, and admissions process review.

### 2. Virtual Open Day: MRes Medical Robotics and Image-Guided Intervention
- **Date:** November 9, 2026 (13:00 - 14:00 GMT)
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Session
- **Target Audience:** Prospective computing and biomedical robotics research applicants
- **Description:** Joint session by the Department of Computing and Institute of Global Health Innovation covering robotic systems, autonomous intervention, hardware-software co-design, and PhD transition routes.

### 3. Faculty of Engineering and Postgraduate Admissions Webinars
- **Dates:** November 9 - 17, 2026 (Multiple sessions across subject areas)
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar Series
- **Target Audience:** International Master's and PhD applicants
- **Description:** Central admissions briefings regarding application procedures, English language compliance, reference requirements, and funding mechanisms.

- **Graduate Application Portal Opens:** Early Autumn 2026
- **President's PhD Scholarships (Round 1 Deadline):** Early November 2026 (~November 1, 2026)
