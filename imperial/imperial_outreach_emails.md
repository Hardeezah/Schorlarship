# Imperial College London PhD Outreach Emails

## 1. Email to Prof. Geoffrey Ye Li
**Focus:** Decentralized Federated Learning and Wireless Connectivity Constraints

**Subject:** Prospective PhD Student (2027) – Decentralized Federated Learning over Constrained Networks

Dear Prof. Li,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining your research group as a PhD student. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My engineering background focuses on building ML systems for unpredictable and severely constrained environments, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. While the edge inference is robust, continuously improving the model with real-world field data across smallholder farmers presents a structural bottleneck: we cannot reliably transmit gigabytes of crop imagery from rural farms over highly unstable 2G/3G networks to a central server.

I have been following your group’s work on Decentralized Federated Learning (DFL) and the intersection of machine learning with wireless communications. The challenge of system scalability and connectivity you address in DFL is exactly the bottleneck I face in agricultural edge deployments. For my PhD, I want to investigate how DFL frameworks can be optimized for environments with severe uplink/downlink asymmetries and high latency, enabling edge nodes to collaboratively train computer vision models without relying on a stable central parameter server.

Given my practical experience deploying offline models and my strong background in mathematics and programming (recognized by my national MATLAB awards), I am deeply interested in the systems-level challenges of wireless FL that your lab focuses on. My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App).

Are you accepting new students for the upcoming cycle, and would investigating DFL under severe agricultural network constraints align with your current research focus?

Thank you for your time,
Hadiza Mohammed
