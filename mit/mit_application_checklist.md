# MIT EECS Direct PhD Application Checklist

This document tracks all required materials, deadlines, and funding details for the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) PhD program.

## Important Dates
- **Application Portal Opens:** September 2026
- **Application Deadline:** December 15, 2026 (for Fall 2027 intake)
- **Outcome Notification:** February to March 2027

## Funding Structure (Fully Funded by Default)
At MIT, the EECS PhD program is **fully funded by default** for all admitted students. 
- **The Process:** You do NOT need to apply for a separate scholarship. If you are admitted to the PhD program, MIT guarantees your funding.
- **What is covered:** This package covers your **full tuition**, **comprehensive medical insurance**, and provides a generous **monthly living stipend** (via fellowships, Research Assistantships, or Teaching Assistantships). For first-year students, funding is guaranteed to give you time to settle into a research group.

## Required Documents Checklist

- [ ] **Statement of Objectives (SOP)**
  - *Requirement:* MIT refers to this as the "Statement of Objectives." You must clearly outline your research experience (Huawei ICT win, CropDiseaseDetector), your proposed research direction (Edge AI/TinyML/Deep Compression), and why MIT (e.g., Prof. Song Han's lab) is the perfect fit.
- [ ] **Curriculum Vitae (CV)**
  - *Status:* Ready (already sent to Ms. Promise). Must highlight your 4.53/5.00 CGPA, MATLAB awards, and engineering experience (KodaTrade).
- [ ] **Academic Transcripts & Degree Certificate**
  - *Requirement:* Official transcripts showing your First Class Honours from Ahmadu Bello University. You CAN apply directly with a Bachelor's degree; a Master's is not required (and is very common for direct admits).
- [ ] **Three (3) Letters of Recommendation**
  - *Requirement:* Standard for US universities. Select the 3 recommenders who can speak most strongly to your technical and research abilities.
- [ ] **Proof of English Proficiency**
  - *Requirement:* Valid IELTS or TOEFL scores. (Check the MIT portal to verify if your English-taught degree from Nigeria exempts you).
- [ ] **GRE Scores**
  - *Requirement:* **NOT REQUIRED.** MIT EECS officially does not use GRE scores for admissions.
