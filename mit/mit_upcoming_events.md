# Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT): Upcoming Admissions Events and Dates

## Overview
The MIT Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) and the MIT Office of Graduate Education (OGE) host virtual admissions panels, information sessions, and workshops each autumn. These sessions focus on direct-from-bachelor's PhD admissions, the Statement of Objectives, faculty advisor matching, and fully funded fellowship structures.

---

## Scheduled Events Schedule (2026/2027 Admissions Cycle)

### 1. MIT EECS Graduate Admissions Virtual Information Sessions
- **Dates:** Mid-October - November 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinar Series & Interactive Q&A Panels
- **Target Audience:** Prospective EECS PhD applicants
- **Description:** Led by EECS faculty and current PhD researchers. Topics include selecting areas of research (Artificial Intelligence and Decision-making, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering), understanding the direct-entry evaluation process, research lab cultures, and how admissions committees evaluate research experience without a master's degree.

### 2. MIT Office of Graduate Education (OGE) Application Workshops
- **Dates:** October 2026
- **Format:** Virtual / Online Webinars
- **Target Audience:** All prospective MIT graduate candidates
- **Description:** Step-by-step guidance on crafting a compelling Statement of Objectives, securing strong recommendation letters, fee waiver requests, and international credential evaluation.
