## 1. Email to Prof. Song Han
**Focus:** Deep Compression, TinyML, and Efficient Inference on Constrained Edge Hardware

**Subject:** Prospective Direct PhD Student (2027) – Edge AI Optimization & Deep Compression for Constrained Environments

Dear Prof. Han,

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining your research group for the Direct PhD program in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) at MIT. I recently graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University (CGPA 4.53/5.00). My engineering background focuses on building and deploying ML systems under severe hardware and energy constraints, work which recently won 1st Place globally at the Huawei ICT Innovation Competition Finals (2025, China).

For that competition, I built CropDiseaseDetector, an agricultural vision model deployed entirely offline on an Orange Pi AIpro. While the software model itself is robust, deploying it to rural smallholder farmers exposed the critical bottleneck of systems efficiency: performing real-time inference without rapidly draining the computational resources and batteries of low-cost edge devices.

I have been closely following your pioneering work on Deep Compression, TinyML, and hardware-aware neural architecture search. The ability to dramatically reduce the size and computational footprint of deep neural networks without sacrificing accuracy is exactly the structural solution required for extreme off-grid deployments. For my PhD, I want to investigate how your lab's compression techniques and efficient inference algorithms can be further optimized for ultra-low-power embedded architectures deployed in highly unpredictable real-world environments.

Given my practical experience deploying offline models and my strong background in systems optimization (recognized by my national MATLAB awards), I am deeply interested in the hardware/software co-design challenges your group tackles. My full project report is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22287430, and the source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd_electron (Edge App) and https://github.com/Hardeezah/cdd-mobile (Mobile App).

Are you accepting new students for the upcoming cycle, and would investigating Deep Compression for extreme resource-constrained edge environments align with your current research focus?

Thank you for your time,
Hadiza Mohammed
